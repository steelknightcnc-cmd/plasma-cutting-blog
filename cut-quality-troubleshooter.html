    Diagnostic discipline

    Why the Order Matters

    01

    Confirm the chart

    Match material, thickness, amperage, process, and consumables to one official mechanized chart row.

    02

    Inspect the system

    Consumables, air, work lead, torch squareness, and motion must be reliable before fine tuning.

    03

    Verify physical height

    Arc voltage is indirect. Measure the physical distance before changing the target.

    04

    Change one variable

    Use repeatable coupons and retain only the adjustment that creates a measurable improvement.
