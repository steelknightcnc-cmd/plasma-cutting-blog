    Important safety and setup note

    Turn off and isolate the plasma system before removing or inspecting consumables. Follow the plasma-source manual for pressure, amperage, consumable selection, pierce height, cut height, and duty-cycle limits. Change one variable at a time and confirm the result with a test coupon.
