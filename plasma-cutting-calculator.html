Calculator instructions

How to Use the CNC Plasma Cutting Calculator

01

Select the material

Choose mild steel, stainless steel, or aluminum. Different materials require different cutting speeds and starting settings.

02

Enter material thickness

Enter the plate or sheet thickness in millimetres or inches. The calculator displays the converted value for comparison.

03

Enter machine capacity

Enter the maximum amperage supported by the plasma cutter. This prevents recommendations beyond the stated machine capacity.

04

Choose a cut priority

Quality favors a more conservative edge-focused result, Balanced combines quality and productivity, and Speed favors a higher production rate.

05

Test and document results

Calculate the settings, make a controlled test cut, and adjust one variable at a time. Print or download the cut card to record proven machine-specific values.

Calculator output

What the Plasma Calculator Provides

Plasma cutting speed

The calculated starting feed rate is displayed in millimetres per minute and inches per minute.

Starting cutting amperage

The suggested cutting current is limited by the selected profile, material thickness, consumable data, and machine capacity.

Air pressure and pierce delay

The results include starting air-pressure and pierce-delay values where supported by the selected machine profile.

Starting cut voltage

A starting voltage may be shown for torch-height-control setup. Confirm the actual cutting height using a test coupon before relying on voltage alone.
