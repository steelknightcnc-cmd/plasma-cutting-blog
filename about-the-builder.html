About the builder

Built from the Shop Floor Up

Plasma Cut Forge was created to turn real CNC plasma experience into practical tools, technical guides, and troubleshooting resources that machine builders and operators can use in the shop.

Hands-on foundation

Welding, Fabrication, and CNC Machine Building

Dom’s work is rooted in hands-on fabrication: fitting and joining material, building machine structures, solving mechanical problems, integrating motion systems, wiring controls, and troubleshooting the details that determine whether a CNC plasma system performs reliably.

Plasma Cut Forge reflects that practical approach. The goal is not to present one universal setting as correct for every machine, but to help users understand the variables, begin with documented information, and improve results through controlled testing.

Why Plasma Cut Forge exists

Useful Information Without Pretending Every Machine Is the Same

Cut quality depends on more than amperage and speed. Torch height, consumable condition, air quality, material condition, work-lead connection, motion stability, and controller behavior can all change the result.

Plasma Cut Forge is being built as a practical resource where operators can calculate starting settings, diagnose cut defects, build machine-specific cut charts, request new machine profiles, and share useful knowledge with the CNC plasma community.
