Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Plasma Cut Forge may process, why it is used, and the choices available to visitors.

Last updated: July 26, 2026

1. Who operates this website

Plasma Cut Forge is operated by Dom. Questions about this Privacy Policy or requests concerning personal information may be submitted through the Contact page.

2. Information that may be collected

The website may process information that you provide directly, including:

  • your name, email address, subject, and message when using the Contact form;
  • machine, torch, documentation, country, and contact information submitted through the machine-profile request form;
  • account, profile, question, answer, comment, and moderation information used by the Community and article-comment systems;
  • feedback submitted through calculator or article-helpfulness features; and
  • any other information you voluntarily include in a submission.

Hosting, database, authentication, and security providers may also process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, timestamps, requested pages, and security logs.

3. Local storage and similar technologies

The website may use browser local storage for limited functions such as repeat-submission controls, remembering whether feedback was submitted, or preserving interface state. Local storage remains on your device until it expires or is removed through your browser settings.

Third-party services may use their own cookies or similar technologies when you visit or interact with their services. Their practices are governed by their own policies.

4. Why information is used

Information may be used to:

  • respond to messages and technical correction reports;
  • review and research requested plasma-cutter profiles;
  • operate accounts, Community questions and answers, and article comments;
  • moderate abuse, spam, unsafe content, and violations of site rules;
  • maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website and its tools;
  • document consent and respond to privacy requests; and
  • comply with legal obligations or protect legal rights.

5. Legal bases for processing

Where the GDPR applies, processing may rely on one or more of the following legal bases: your consent; steps taken at your request; performance of a service you requested; legitimate interests in operating, securing, moderating, and improving the website; and compliance with legal obligations.

You may withdraw consent for future processing where consent is the applicable legal basis. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

6. Service providers and external services

The website currently relies on or links to services that may include:

  • GitHub Pages for website hosting and delivery;
  • Supabase for database, authentication, submissions, and Community features;
  • Google Fonts for delivery of website typefaces;
  • Ko-fi for optional financial support; and
  • manufacturer, retailer, documentation, social-media, or other third-party links.

These providers may process information under their own privacy terms. Depending on the provider and its configuration, information may be processed outside your country or outside the European Economic Area using the safeguards available to that provider.

7. Public content

Community questions, answers, display names, and article comments may be visible publicly. Do not publish private contact details, passwords, confidential business information, or information about another person without permission.

8. Retention

Contact messages and machine-profile requests may be retained while they are reviewed and for a reasonable period afterward for follow-up, security, recordkeeping, and dispute prevention. Community content may remain available until it is deleted, anonymized, or removed through moderation. Provider logs are retained according to each provider’s settings and policies.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection to processing, and to withdraw consent. Identity verification may be required before a request is completed.

Submit a request through the Contact page and select Privacy or data request.

Individuals may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority. In Austria, the supervisory authority is the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde).

10. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect submissions, including private database tables and access-control policies. No internet transmission, browser, hosting platform, or database can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Children

Plasma Cut Forge is intended for adults and technically supervised users. The website is not directed to children, and children should not submit personal information without the involvement of a parent or legal guardian.

12. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when site features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The date at the top of the page identifies the latest published version.
