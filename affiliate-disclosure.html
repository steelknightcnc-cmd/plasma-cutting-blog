Affiliate Disclosure

This page explains how future affiliate links, sponsored content, free products, and other material connections will be disclosed.

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Future Affiliate Links

Plasma Cut Forge may use affiliate links in future product guides, equipment comparisons, tool lists, or related content. An affiliate link may result in Plasma Cut Forge receiving a commission when a visitor makes a qualifying purchase, generally without increasing the visitor’s purchase price.

At the time this disclosure was published, a link should not be assumed to be an affiliate link unless it is clearly identified as one.

1. Clear disclosure near recommendations

When affiliate links are used, Plasma Cut Forge intends to place a clear disclosure near the relevant recommendation or link. This standalone page supplements those disclosures; it is not intended to replace a clear, conspicuous disclosure where the recommendation is presented.

2. Editorial independence

Affiliate compensation does not guarantee favorable coverage, a recommendation, inclusion in a guide, or a particular ranking. The goal is to describe products based on available documentation, practical relevance, testing where possible, and honest assessment.

3. No guarantee of product suitability

Product links and recommendations are not guarantees that a product is safe, compatible, available, authentic, legally permitted, or suitable for your machine. Verify current specifications, seller information, warranties, return terms, electrical ratings, certification, and manufacturer instructions before purchasing or installing equipment.

4. Prices and availability

Prices, inventory, shipping, taxes, regional availability, and seller terms may change without notice. The retailer’s checkout page and terms control the final transaction.

5. Sponsored content and free products

Any sponsored content, paid placement, free product, discount, commercial relationship, or other material connection will be disclosed clearly in the relevant content.

6. Questions

Questions about a product relationship or affiliate disclosure may be submitted through the Contact page.
