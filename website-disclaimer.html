Calculator Results Are Starting Recommendations

Plasma Cut Forge calculator results—including feed rate, cutting current, gas pressure, pierce delay, cut voltage, and related values— are starting recommendations only. They are not guaranteed factory settings and do not guarantee cut quality, consumable life, machine compatibility, or safe operation.

Always compare results with the current manual and official mechanized cut chart for your exact plasma source, torch, consumables, material, amperage, and process. Use controlled test coupons before production. When information conflicts, the machine manufacturer’s instructions and qualified professional guidance take priority.

Plasma Cut Forge provides general educational and informational material about CNC plasma cutting, welding, fabrication, electronics, controls, and machine troubleshooting. Content is not a substitute for professional engineering, electrical, welding, safety, legal, medical, or manufacturer-specific advice.

2. Machine and process variation

Plasma-cutting results vary with torch height, consumable condition, material chemistry and surface condition, air quality and flow, motion accuracy, work-lead connection, electrical noise, controller behavior, ambient conditions, operator technique, and many other variables. Settings that work on one machine may be unsuitable or unsafe on another.

3. Hazardous equipment

CNC plasma systems, welding equipment, machine tools, compressed gas, pneumatic systems, high-current circuits, motion systems, and mains electricity can cause fire, electric shock, burns, eye injury, respiratory exposure, crushing, entanglement, property damage, serious injury, or death.

Use appropriate personal protective equipment, ventilation, grounding, guarding, fire prevention, lockout/tagout procedures, and qualified assistance. Never bypass safety interlocks or work on energized equipment unless the task is lawful, necessary, and performed by a properly qualified person using an approved procedure.
