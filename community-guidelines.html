Steel Knight CNC Community

Community Guidelines

Keep the community useful, respectful, technically honest, and safe.

1. Stay related to CNC and fabrication

Topics include plasma, mills, routers, lathes, motion control, CAD/CAM, electronics, welding, tooling, and related software.

2. Treat members with respect

Debate ideas, not people. Harassment, threats, insults, vulgar abuse, hate speech, and discriminatory slurs are prohibited.

3. Put safety first

Clearly identify hazards involving mains voltage, plasma arc voltage, fumes, lasers, rotating machinery, pressure, fire, or stored energy. Do not encourage bypassing safety devices.

4. Give technically honest answers

Separate tested facts from estimates and personal experience. Include machine models, materials, settings, software versions, and sources when they matter.

5. No weapon-building or illegal activity

Do not use the community to design, manufacture, modify, or evade controls for weapons, prohibited components, or illegal items.

6. No spam or deceptive promotion

Relevant recommendations are welcome when they answer the question and disclose business relationships. Repetitive promotion and scams are prohibited.

7. Protect personal information

Do not publish private addresses, phone numbers, credentials, confidential drawings, or another person's identifying details without permission.

8. Make questions useful

Use a descriptive title and include the machine, controller, software, material, settings, symptoms, errors, and troubleshooting already attempted.

9. Report problems instead of escalating

Use the Report button for abusive language, unsafe guidance, spam, personal information, or off-topic content.

10. Moderation

Posts appear immediately but may later be hidden, locked, or removed. Repeated or serious violations may result in account restrictions.

Automatic wording check

The form warns about common vulgar wording before submission. Automated filters are imperfect, so reports and human moderation remain important.
