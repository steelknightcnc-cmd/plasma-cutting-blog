Air Compressor Sizing for Plasma Cutters

Start with the cutter manufacturer’s official SCFM at PSI requirement. The compressor pump must sustain the airflow; the tank only provides temporary reserve.

Size My Compressor Buying Checklist
Fast answer Find official SCFM and PSI. Add operating margin. Compare delivered SCFM at pressure. Then evaluate tank and duty cycle.
Do not size from amperage alone.

Use the exact operator manual for the plasma cutter model, torch, and process.

    Core concept

    SCFM Sustains the Cut; the Tank Delays the Shortage

    Delivered SCFM

    The pump must meet total airflow demand at the required operating pressure.

    Tank capacity

    A larger tank extends short cuts and reduces cycling but cannot permanently fix an undersized pump.

    Long-cut reliability

    Duty Cycle Matters

    Check rated duty cycle

    Confirm the compressor can run through long CNC contours.

    Use delivered SCFM

    Do not use displacement or peak CFM.

    Include other loads

    Add dryers, blow guns, and simultaneous shop tools.

    Check cooling

    Hot rooms and poor ventilation reduce reliability.

    Protect delivered pressure

    Hose, Couplers, Filters, and Regulators

    Measure under flow

    Static tank pressure does not prove pressure at the cutter.

    Avoid restrictive couplers

    Small fittings can choke a large compressor.

    Account for long runs

    Distance, bends, and small internal diameter increase pressure loss.

    Inspect filter drop

    Saturated or undersized filters can starve the torch.

    Consumable protection

    Clean, Dry, Oil-Free Air

    Moisture and oil can destabilize the arc and shorten consumable life. Drain the receiver, allow air to cool, use correctly sized separation and filtration, and verify pressure at the cutter while air is flowing.

    FAQ

    Common Compressor Questions

    Is tank size or SCFM more important?

    SCFM determines sustained airflow. Tank size provides temporary reserve.

    Can an auxiliary tank fix low output?

    It increases stored air but does not eliminate an ongoing pump deficit.

    Why does pressure fall only while cutting?

    Hose, fittings, filters, or the pump may be unable to deliver enough air under flow.
