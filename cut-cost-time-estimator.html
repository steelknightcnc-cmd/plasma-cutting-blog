How the estimate is calculated

Torch-on time is cut length divided by cutting speed. Pierce time is added for every contour, then multiplied by quantity. Machine rate is applied to cutting, piercing, and setup time. Consumables and optional material cost are added separately so you can match the way your shop quotes work.
